**Project:** RadioText AI, Android application, version **0.2.0 Beta**

## Publicly documented concept and distinctive feature

RadioText AI combines live internet-radio playback with on-device Russian/English speech-to-text recognition (Vosk) and an **experimental radio-contest keyword detection** feature.

The project direction includes helping listeners notice speech about **radio contests, keywords, and prizes** while listening to a station. The current beta publicly documents experimental keyword detection; this record **does not claim** that reliable contest discovery, automatic entry, prize verification, or prize winning has been demonstrated.

This combination and its development direction are documented here to create a dated, public record of the author's project and stated feature concept. It is not a claim to have invented speech recognition, radio competitions, keyword spotting, or every possible implementation of these ideas.
