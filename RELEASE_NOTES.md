# RadioText AI 0.2.0 — Public Beta

**Android | Free | Experimental beta**

RadioText AI brings internet-radio playback and live speech-to-text captions to Android. It uses Vosk for Russian and English recognition and includes experimental keyword detection.

## Features
- Internet radio playback
- Live RU/EN transcription with Vosk
- Experimental keyword detection
- Adjustable caption text

## Important limitations
Transcriptions may contain errors, delays or missing words. Station availability and Android capture permissions vary. TalkBack and 200% system font scaling have not been fully verified. Not intended for emergency or safety-critical communication.
